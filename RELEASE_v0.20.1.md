## Outcome

The live v0.20 Gmail transport remains intact. This coordinated follow-up connects
the Workspace aliases to Control Center operations: Gmail labels and filters are
provisioned safely, Shield verdicts flow back to Gmail, and per-alias automatic
acknowledgments run behind Shield and loop-prevention gates.

- Eight department labels and alias-routing Gmail filters.
- Four Gmail-visible Shield verdict labels.
- Gmail Spam ingestion and a Control Center Spam folder.
- Per-alias 24/7 acknowledgment templates with durable one-per-Gmail-conversation
  dedupe and no first-deploy replies to older inbox history.
- Auto-response protection for spam, review, lists, bulk mail, automated senders,
  no-reply senders, and Pitmark-originated messages.
- Desktop and mobile Auto Reply controls backed by the same Cloud APIs.
- Restored desktop sidebar with cache-busted, authoritative rail styling.
- Exact Google Cloud OAuth and Render setup instructions.
