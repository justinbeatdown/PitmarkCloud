# Pitmark Motorsports — Logo Usage Guide v1
Updated October 22, 2026. Brand division of Pitmark Racing Co.

## Identity
Official name: Pitmark Motorsports. Planned public team launch: January 2027.
Colors: Pitmark Orange #FF5500, black #111111 and white #FFFFFF. Visual direction: premium, modern, clean white layouts with restrained orange accent.

## Primary logo — clean wordmark
Use the **unoutlined transparent** approved PITMARK MOTORSPORTS wordmark in the website navigation and other clean white/light-background corporate contexts.
CDN: https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/1067/3913/8641/files/pitmark-motorsports-clean-header-wordmark.png?v=1792711927

## Secondary logo — white circular badge
Use the circular white-interior logo in hero displays, social avatars, Discord icons, round patches, and favicons. Keep white **inside the circle**; the area outside the circle is transparent.
CDN: https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/1067/3913/8641/files/pitmark-motorsports-official-circular-badge.png?v=1792711547

## Supporting logo — outlined wordmark
Use the outlined transparent wordmark where needed for busy backgrounds, vehicle decals, dark sections, and merchandise. Do not use this as the default white website header mark.
CDN: https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/1067/3913/8641/files/pitmark-motorsports-official-wordmark.png?v=1792711542

## Operational rules
- Preserve aspect ratios, safe spacing, and approved artwork.
- Never reconstruct logos using a generic font or placeholder.
- Choose sufficient contrast; preview against both white and black backgrounds.
- Do not imply racing divisions, entries, or individual sponsorships are active until authorized.
- Never include a driver's outside sponsor mark without permission.
- The Motorsports team remains separate from PRT software and the Pitmark Racing League.
- Do not configure motorsports.pitmarkracing.com until the owner approves domain launch.
