BEHIND THE NAME.

Adrian “Pipe” Featherston brings perspective from both sides of the racing world. He competes in UMP Modified racing, including time behind the wheel of his own PRC A-Mod, while staying active in the sim-racing community.

As a manager and setup engineer with Honor First Racing, Adrian works on race preparation and development. His experience with dirt modified racing and iRacing adds a valuable technical perspective to Pitmark Motorsports. Existing commitments, sponsors and affiliations remain his own.
