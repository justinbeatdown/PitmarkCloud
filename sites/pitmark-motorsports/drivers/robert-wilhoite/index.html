BEHIND THE NAME.

Robert “Lil Raw” Wilhoite carries a family racing legacy into iRacing. The No. 15 means more than a number: it is part of the personal story and history that connect him to the sport.

Robert's journey includes Daytona racing experiences and a strong connection to the sim-racing community. As Pitmark Motorsports' first confirmed founding driver, he helps establish the values behind the program: commitment, identity and respect for the people who make racing meaningful.
