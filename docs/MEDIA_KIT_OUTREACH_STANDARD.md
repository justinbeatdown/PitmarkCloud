# Pitmark Media Kit — Outreach & Partnership Standard

**Canonical media kit:** https://links.pitmarkracing.com/media-kit

Pitmark Racing Co. treats the Media + Partner Kit as a first-class sales, credibility, partnership and company-introduction asset. It should not be treated as a hidden link or optional extra.

## Default use

Include the media kit whenever someone asks:
- Who is Pitmark?
- What does Pitmark do?
- What can Pitmark offer?
- How can we work together?
- Do you have partnership, media, sponsorship or collaboration information?
- Can I send this to my team / owner / promoter / marketing person?
- Do you have anything I can review before a call?

Also include it proactively when appropriate in:
- brand and manufacturer outreach
- track, series and promoter outreach
- broadcast/media outreach
- creator/influencer outreach
- driver/team relationship building
- PRT / Race Center partnership conversations
- sponsorship and promotional-partner discussions
- post-call recaps when the recipient may share Pitmark internally

Do not force the link into ordinary support replies, transactional messages or casual conversation where it adds no value.

## Standard pitch pattern

Keep the email or DM personal and specific first. Use the media kit as the handoff, not as a replacement for the pitch.

Recommended structure:
1. Why Pitmark is reaching out to this specific person/company.
2. The specific overlap or opportunity.
3. One concise sentence explaining Pitmark.
4. Media Kit link for the full picture.
5. Low-friction next step.

Recommended line:

> If it helps, our Media + Partner Kit gives the quickest full picture of Pitmark, what we're building, and the different ways we work with people in racing: https://links.pitmarkracing.com/media-kit

## Partnership package

When a prospect progresses beyond first contact, the default Pitmark partnership stack is:
- Personalized email / DM / call recap
- Media + Partner Kit
- Relevant product surface: Race Center, PRT, Racing Desk, store or other specific project
- Native **Work With Pitmark** intake when structured follow-up is useful
- Any person-specific proposal, deck, agreement or documentation required for the opportunity

## Native intake

The **Work With Pitmark** CTA on the Media Kit must use Pitmark's first-party intake flow rather than a Google Form.

Submissions should route directly to Pitmark's outreach/partnership inbox with:
- partner type
- opportunity type
- organization/project
- contact name and email
- website/social
- reference/deck URL
- opportunity details
- permission to contact

## Website placement

The Media Kit should be discoverable from:
- Racing Desk
- Race Center footer
- Pitmark storefront/footer or business/company navigation where practical
- relevant partnership/contact surfaces

## Quality rule

The media kit is a credibility asset. Keep it current with major Pitmark products, positioning and contact information. Any future material change to Pitmark's core offering should trigger a review of the Media Kit.
