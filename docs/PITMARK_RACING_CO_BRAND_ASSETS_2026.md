## Brand palette / presentation
- Pitmark orange `#FF5500`; black; white; charcoal.
- Preserve native aspect ratio, transparency, safe margins and all lettering.
- On light backgrounds, use enough black/charcoal contrast around the mark. On dark backgrounds, keep readable white/silver.
- Do not convert an official logo to a flat generic-font approximation or overlay other sponsors.
- No need to rebrand archive editorial photos, old race documentation, signed agreements, or licensed partner art.
- Parent company logos are **not** replacements for Pitmark Racing Tools (PRT), Pitmark Racing League (PRL), or Pitmark Motorsports' separate identity.

## Integration status and limits
- `api/media_kit.html`, `api/partner_guide.html`, and `api/links.html` use these image URLs.
- Public legacy logo routes in `api/links_ui.py` redirect to the corresponding current assets.
- Shopify Files contains all three masters; Shopify's **published theme logo and favicon must be changed separately in the theme editor** where live-theme writes aren't available.
- External platform avatars, Canva designs, pre-rendered banners and printed merchandise need a separate update and visual review, not an automatic silent swap.
