# Pitmark Cloud v0.14.6 — Real-Time Social Timeline + Instagram Asset Workflow

- Native Facebook/Instagram Social Post Composer in Control Center.
- Instagram image modes: automatic Pitmark Asset Library selection or direct image upload.
- Uploaded images are served from a stable public Pitmark Cloud asset URL; optional reusable asset-library ingestion.
- Social Timeline is ordered by source/event time, not discovery time.
- NOW / RECENT / SCHEDULED / PUBLISHED / EXPIRED timeline lanes.
- Reactive intelligence posts expire after the configured real-time window (default 4 hours) and are blocked server-side from late publishing.
- Autopilot scans current racing signals every 15 minutes by default; older useful opportunities remain background/long-form candidates rather than fake breaking social posts.
- Blog workflow remains independent and suitable for longer-form, slower-cycle content.
- Release version source of truth: 0.14.6.
