# Pitmark Cloud v0.16.15 — Support Mail Identity

Adds `support@mail.pitmarkracing.com` as an approved Pitmark Mail identity.

- Available automatically in the existing desktop From selector.
- Available automatically in the existing mobile From selector.
- Supports outbound sending through the existing Resend configuration.
- Replies to mail received at the Support identity automatically inherit the Support sender.
- Drafts preserve the Support identity.
- No DNS, webhook, database, Shopify, Shield, Blog, or source-reader changes.
