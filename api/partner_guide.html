Your first-lap checklist

01

Accept your invitation

If your partnership includes Shopify Collabs, accept the email invite and finish your creator profile. League, track and other partner arrangements may use a different setup.

02

Join the server, then make sure Pitmark knows your Discord username. Approved partners can be assigned the Official Partner role and private Partner Lounge access.

03

Grab your link + code

Use the exact affiliate link and discount code assigned to you. Your commission, customer offer and payout terms are the ones shown in your individual agreement or Collabs account.

04

Download approved assets

Use the current Pitmark logos below. Keep proportions, colors and artwork intact. If you need a custom co-branded graphic, ask us instead of rebuilding the logo.

05

Make your first post

Show Pitmark in context: race night, the sim rig, your league, a product you actually wear, or a PRT feature you actually use. Tag Pitmark and include your link or code where it makes sense.

06

Send us what you make

Drop strong posts, photos and clips in Discord so Pitmark can see them, support them and potentially reshare them.

AFFILIATE + COLLABS

Your link is the engine. Your voice is the fuel.

Some Pitmark partnerships include Shopify Collabs affiliate tracking. If yours does, your dashboard is where you can find your personal link, discount offer, commission details and performance information.

  • Use your assigned code exactly as provided.
  • Use your personal affiliate link whenever a clickable link fits the platform.
  • Clearly disclose when you may earn commission from a purchase.
  • Do not post Pitmark codes to mass coupon-code sites or spam unrelated communities.
  • Do not promise a discount, payout or perk that is not shown in your current program.

PITMARK RACING TOOLS

If you post PRT, keep the app as the source of truth.

PRT evolves quickly. Show real current app footage, overlays and features. Do not recreate telemetry, standings, warnings or other app information in a way that changes what the software actually displayed.

KEEP IT CLEAN

A few partner guardrails

Don't impersonate Pitmark

You are a Pitmark partner, not an employee or official company account unless Pitmark has specifically told you otherwise.

Don't make up claims

Keep product, pricing, PRT feature, giveaway and promotion claims accurate and current.

Get permission

Use photos, music, race footage, logos and other intellectual property you own or have permission to use.

Ask before paid ads

Do not run paid advertising using the Pitmark name, trademarks or partner offer without approval from Pitmark.

Don't abuse the offer

No fake orders, self-referral schemes, coupon-site dumping or other attempts to manipulate tracked sales.

When in doubt, send it

If a post, co-branded graphic or promotion feels questionable, send it to the Partnerships team before publishing.

QUICK ANSWERS

Partner FAQ

Can I make my own Pitmark graphics?

Yes. Keep official Pitmark marks unchanged and use assets you have permission to use. For major co-branded graphics, event art or anything that changes how Pitmark is represented, send a draft to Partnerships first.

Where do I see my affiliate stats?

If your program uses Shopify Collabs, use your Collabs creator dashboard. Your individual program controls the commission and customer offer that apply to your account.

What if I don't have a discount code?

Not every partnership needs one. Use the benefits and links listed in your individual agreement. If something appears to be missing, contact Partnerships.

How do I get the private Discord partner channels?

Join Pitmark Discord, then send your Discord username to Partnerships. Approved partners can be assigned the Official Partner role that unlocks the Partner Lounge.

Can Pitmark reshare my content?

Tag Pitmark and drop strong content in the appropriate Discord partner or showcase channel. If you do not want something reshared, say so when you send it.
