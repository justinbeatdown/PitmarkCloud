PARTNERSHIP LANES

Not every partner needs the same deal.

Your relationship with Pitmark should fit what you actually do in racing. Some partners receive affiliate links. Some get event or league support. Some collaborate on content, products or PRT. Some do a mix. Your individual agreement always controls the exact benefits and expectations.

IF YOUR DEAL INCLUDES AFFILIATE SALES

Shopify Collabs & partner codes

Affiliate tracking is one tool inside the larger Pitmark partnership program. Drivers, creators, leagues and other partners may receive a personal link or discount code when it fits the relationship.

  • Use the exact link and code assigned to you.
  • Your individual program determines your commission, customer discount and payout terms.
  • Clearly disclose when you may earn commission from a purchase.
  • Do not dump Pitmark codes onto mass coupon sites or unrelated communities.
  • Do not promise discounts, freebies or payouts that are not part of your actual program.

BEST USE OF A PARTNER CODE

Give it context.

“I wear this at the track,” “this is the gear I'm using,” or “here's the Pitmark stuff our league is working with” is stronger than dropping a naked discount code every few days.

FOR PRT / SIM-TOOL PARTNERS

Keep the software as the source of truth.

This section only applies when your Pitmark relationship includes Pitmark Racing Tools. PRT evolves quickly, so use current real app footage, overlays and features. Never recreate telemetry, standings, warnings or other app information in a way that changes what the software actually displayed.

PRT HOME ↗ REPORT / DISCUSS IN DISCORD ↗

PRT CONTENT CHECK

  • Is the footage current?
  • Are telemetry and overlays unaltered?
  • Are you describing features that actually exist?
  • If something looks broken, report it instead of guessing.

KEEP IT CLEAN

A few partner guardrails

Be clear about the relationship

You are a Pitmark partner, not an employee or official company account unless Pitmark has specifically told you otherwise.

Don't make up claims

Keep product, pricing, PRT feature, giveaway, event and promotion claims accurate and current.

Get permission

Use photos, music, race footage, logos and other intellectual property you own or have permission to use.

Ask before paid ads

Do not run paid advertising using the Pitmark name, trademarks or partner offer without approval from Pitmark.

Don't game tracking

No fake orders, self-referral schemes, coupon-site dumping or other attempts to manipulate tracked sales.

When in doubt, send it

If a co-branded graphic, promotion or claim feels questionable, send it to the Partnerships team before publishing.

QUICK ANSWERS

Partner FAQ

Does every partner get an affiliate code?

No. Affiliate sales are only one type of partnership benefit. A track, league, driver, creator or business collaboration may be structured completely differently depending on what makes sense.

Can real-world drivers and teams partner with Pitmark?

Absolutely. Pitmark is built around racing culture, not just sim racing. Grassroots drivers and teams are one of the core partnership lanes.

Can I make my own Pitmark graphics?

Yes. Keep official Pitmark marks unchanged and use assets you have permission to use. For major co-branded graphics, event art or anything that changes how Pitmark is represented, send a draft to Partnerships first.

Where do I see my affiliate stats?

If your program uses Shopify Collabs, use your Collabs creator dashboard. Your individual program controls the commission and customer offer that apply to your account.

How do I get the private Discord partner channels?

Join Pitmark Discord, then send your Discord username to Partnerships. Approved partners can be assigned the Official Partner role that unlocks the Partner Lounge.

Can Pitmark reshare my content?

Tag Pitmark and drop strong content in the appropriate Discord partner or showcase channel. If you do not want something reshared, say so when you send it.
