# Rookie Year Campaign V1

Pitmark Cloud v0.12.3 adds the first durable Campaign Manager workflow, with Rookie Year 2026 as the flagship campaign.

Pipeline: Prospect → Interested → Intake Sent → Submitted → Verification → Ready for Review → Selected → Story Building → Racer Review → Scheduled → Published → Alumni.

Safety rules: media permission and guardian state are stored independently; public publishing remains approval-first. Research & Prepare only queues research and never contacts a racer automatically.
